Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026 | Effective: January 2026

🌿 Privacy Summary

  • We DO: Encrypt your data, delete it when you ask, and limit third-party access
  • We DON'T: Sell your data, train AI on your health information, or share with advertisers
  • You control: Your data, your privacy settings, and your ability to delete everything
⚠️ Important: KWO Recovery is NOT a medical provider and is NOT HIPAA-compliant. We take privacy seriously, but we cannot guarantee the same protections as a doctor's office. If you need medical help, please contact a licensed professional.

1. What Data We Collect

Health & Recovery Data

  • Daily check-in responses (mood ratings, emotions, activities)
  • Recovery streak information
  • Challenge completions and points
  • Crisis session access (timestamps only)

AI Chat Data

  • Your messages and AI responses
  • Messages are sent to OpenAI for processing
  • OpenAI retains data for 30 days (abuse monitoring)
  • OpenAI does NOT train models on API data

Account Information

  • Email address (for account recovery)
  • Password (hashed - we can't see it)
  • Subscription status via RevenueCat

What We DON'T Collect

  • ❌ Location data
  • ❌ Contacts or messages
  • ❌ Photos or files
  • ❌ Data if you disable analytics

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Calculate recovery streaks and progression
  • Generate personalized recommendations
  • Deliver crisis support resources
  • Improve app features (anonymized)
  • Send notifications you've opted into

3. Third-Party Services

Service What's Shared Their Policy
OpenAI Chat messages (processed, 30-day retention) Does NOT train on API data
RevenueCat Subscription status only No health data access
ElevenLabs Voice text for synthesis Audio not stored

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Data Type Retention
Chat messages 90 days (then auto-deleted)
Check-in data Duration of account
Analytics logs 30 days

Delete Your Data

  1. Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account
  2. Or email kwofitnesltd@outlook.com
  3. We'll delete everything within 30 days

5. Security Measures

  • ✅ TLS 1.3 encryption in transit
  • ✅ AES-256 encryption at rest
  • ✅ Rate limiting on all endpoints
  • ✅ API keys stored on backend only
  • ✅ Continuous security monitoring

6. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA)

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Correction: Fix inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Delete all your data
  • Portability: Download your data (JSON)
  • Opt-out: Disable analytics anytime

7. Children's Privacy

KWO Recovery is for users 18+ only.
We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you're under 18 and struggling, please contact SAMHSA at 1-800-662-4357.

8. Policy Updates

We'll notify you via email and in-app notification 30 days before material changes. Continued use after changes means acceptance.
